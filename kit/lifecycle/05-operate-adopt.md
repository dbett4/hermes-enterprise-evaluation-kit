# Stage 5 — Operate & Adopt

**Status:** frozen vendor-neutral kernel

**Purpose:** operate inside the granted envelope and transfer practical ownership to the organization.

## Entry conditions

- Accepted Stage 4 authority decision
- Named operating owner, support route, stop authority, and evidence route
- Approved configuration identity and tested recovery procedure

## Required questions

1. Is each run inside the authorized action, resource, environment, data, configuration, duration, and cost boundaries?
2. Do target-system effects, evidence population, quality, latency, cost, and exception metrics reconcile to the run record?
3. Can operators challenge, pause, stop, recover, and escalate without hidden builder knowledge?
4. Have incidents, repeated denials, approval fatigue, drift, supplier change, or orphaned identity altered the risk tier or authority?
5. Are training, support, ownership, recertification, and adoption obligations being met?
6. Does the evidence support continuation, contraction, suspension, or a bounded promotion recommendation?

## Decision rules

1. Execute only the fixed authorized configuration. Drift or a material change stops the affected scope and returns to Stage 3 or 4.
2. Reconcile every material run through authorized intent, observed target effect, and independent disposition. Keep open obligations explicit.
3. Contain incidents first, preserve evidence, contract the proven common-mode scope, recover through a tested path, and install the narrowest prevention or early-detection guard.
4. Repeated denial, bypass attempts, stale recertification, orphaned identity, failed stop/readback, or acceptance regression suspends or narrows the affected authority.
5. Performance may support a promotion recommendation; it never self-expands authority. A policy owner must ratify the precise new bound.
6. Handoff is incomplete until the receiving owner can operate, challenge, stop, recover, and escalate without the builder.

## Outputs

- Run receipts and target-effect reconciliation
- Service, quality, cost, adoption, and approval-performance measures
- Incident, denial, exception, drift, and change records
- Promotion, contraction, suspension, and remediation recommendations
- Operator training, tabletop, support, and owner-acceptance evidence
- Open-obligation and recertification register

## Accountable owner

The operating owner owns service operation, incident escalation, exceptions, and adoption. Policy, evidence, system, and support roles retain their named responsibilities but do not displace operating accountability.

## Exceptions and escalation

Emergency continuity may use only a predeclared, tested path with an owner, trigger, deadline, compensating control, and stop mechanism. An improvised workaround cannot expand authority. Uncontained effect, lost readback, or unknown configuration state suspends the affected lane and escalates.

## Exit gate and acceptance tests

`continue` passes only while:

- observed operation stays inside the authority and configuration identity;
- mandatory quality, evidence, continuity, cost, and ownership measures remain within thresholds;
- incidents and exceptions have dispositions and owners; and
- operators can demonstrate the stop, recovery, challenge, and escalation paths.

Negative test: an apparently successful run with a target effect outside the granted resource scope must contract or suspend the affected authority, never count toward promotion.
