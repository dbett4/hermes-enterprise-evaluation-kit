# Adoption and ownership

**Status:** frozen vendor-neutral kernel

## Risk signals — pay extra attention when

No operating owner, builder-only knowledge, multi-team handoff, unclear policy or support ownership, weak challenge/override path, staff or role change, long-lived operation, missing training, or unresolved transfer/retirement obligations.

## Control rules

- Name outcome, policy, operating, system, evidence, verifier, approver, support, incident, transfer, and retirement ownership; one person may hold several roles only when the independence claim is adjusted honestly.
- Supply a runbook, training, challenge, stop, recovery, escalation, support, and exception route.
- Prove ownership through a tabletop or observed operation, not document existence alone.
- Keep accountability with the current owner until the receiving owner accepts the exact authority, configuration, evidence, and open obligations.
- Record adoption friction and hidden dependencies; do not treat usage count as ownership.

## Implementation slots — where controls can live

Human operating procedure, training/support system, governance process, incident route, access lifecycle, and transfer/retirement process.

## Required evidence — what to keep

RACI or authority records; owner acceptance; runbook; training/tabletop result; support and escalation route; challenge/stop/recovery demonstration; open-obligation register; transfer or retirement acceptance.

## Metrics and triggers — what to watch

Unowned critical roles = 0; failed stop/handoff table-tops = 0. Track training completion, time to competent operation, support burden, override/challenge use, exception age, owner vacancies, and transfer defects. Owner loss or failed stop capability suspends or narrows operation.

## Tier application — depth by risk

- L1: named owners, basic runbook, support route, and explicit retirement responsibility.
- L2: training, tabletop, challenge/override path, adoption measures, and receiving-owner acceptance.
- L3: role separation where required, succession and recertification, operational exercises, independent ownership review, and formal transfer/retirement gates.

## Exit rule — done when

No handoff completes until the receiving owner can operate, challenge, stop, recover, escalate, and transfer or retire the deployment without hidden builder knowledge.
